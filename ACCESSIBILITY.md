# Accessibility

GOLD aims to make its documentation and agent guidance understandable and usable by people with different access needs. Contributions that remove barriers are welcome.

## Scope and current limitations

GOLD provides text-based presets, Markdown documentation and instruction resources. It does not implement the DeepSeek Harness interface, browser, terminal or assistive technology integration. Accessibility of those surfaces depends on the host application and tools.

No formal WCAG conformance audit or comprehensive screen-reader/platform compatibility test has been completed for GOLD. Diagrams and wide tables may be difficult to navigate in some renderers; explanatory text should accompany essential visual information. AI-generated output can vary and is not guaranteed to follow every requested format.

## Guidance for contributors

- Use descriptive headings, clear language and meaningful link labels.
- Explain abbreviations and preserve a logical reading order.
- Provide text equivalents for essential diagrams/images; do not communicate meaning only through color.
- Prefer readable examples and concise tables; avoid unnecessary visual formatting.
- Honor user requests for simpler language, plain-text structure or reduced formatting where feasible.

## Report a barrier

Open a bug report describing the document or interaction, expected behavior, observed barrier and relevant renderer/browser/assistive technology versions. Sharing disability or medical information is not required. For a private report, email [bigun.md@gmail.com](mailto:bigun.md@gmail.com).

Reports are reviewed alongside other usability issues; no remediation timeline is guaranteed. Host-interface issues may be referred to the Harness project with the reporter's consent before forwarding private details.
